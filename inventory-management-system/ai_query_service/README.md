# AI Query Service

## Supported questions

The AI service supports:

### Product information

Examples:
- What is the price of the Laptop?
- Give me details about product 1.

### Product availability

Examples:
- Where is the Laptop available?
- Which branches have this product?

### Branch stock

Examples:
- What products are available in Dijon branch?

### Shopping list

Examples:
- Which branch can satisfy:
  - 2 Laptops
  - 1 Mouse

## Unsupported questions

The service refuses questions unrelated to inventory,
products or branches.
